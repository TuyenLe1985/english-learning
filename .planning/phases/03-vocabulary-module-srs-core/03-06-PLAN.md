| T-03-16 | Elevation of Privilege | srs/queue, srs/review, my-words relays | mitigate | Every relay calls auth() and returns 401 before proxying; middleware also protects /review and /vocabulary; NestJS enforces JwtAuthGuard + userId scoping |
| T-03-17 | Information Disclosure | review queue + my-words | mitigate | NestJS scopes all queries to req.user.userId; relays never forward a userId; a user only ever sees their own cards/words |
| T-03-18 | Tampering | review submission | mitigate | NestJS ReviewSubmitSchema (Zod) restricts rating to the 4 enum values; cardId validated against userId-scoped findFirst |
